Diversity and inclusion
Connecting people, ideas and capital with our borderless approach to banking.


As a bank, we’re all about the people we serve. They’re at the heart of our business, at the forefront of our minds every decision we make. Our purpose is to connect them with ideas and opportunities across borders, continents and cultures. And we carry that through into how we communicate by being inclusive in our language and choice of imagery.

We never forget our customers are real people with diverse beliefs, feelings and identities. So, no matter who or where they are, we talk to them in a way that makes them feel seen and respected. We feel this should be the norm, and we hope to lead by example and encourage other organisations to adopt similar practices.
