This guide was created as a resource for UC Davis Development and Alumni Relations (DEVAR) staff to support our division’s ongoing commitment to advancing principles of diversity, equity and inclusion in every aspect of our work. It outlines guiding principles for choosing language that reflects diverse perspectives and seeks to make communication more inclusive and more equitable for all.

For UC Davis and our DEVAR team, communicating in inclusive ways is critical to the relationships we build with every donor, partner, patient and constituent. This guide is intended to support every staff member in our daily work as fundraising professionals and as members of our university community.
