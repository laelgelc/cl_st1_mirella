EDI Considerations

The following resource has been created to support groups to ensure EDI considerations are embedded across their work and to be used as an ongoing tool. 

Please review the information below and adapt to best meet your group's needs. Depending on the nature of your group, not all of the 'Types of Task' or suggestions will be relevant, so you may wish to delete them or amend the wording to better suit your focus.

The strands listed below are general considerations and may look different in practice for intersectional groups. Most considerations relate to a specific EDI strand stated in the left-hand column, while some are relevant across all EDI strands ("All Strands").
