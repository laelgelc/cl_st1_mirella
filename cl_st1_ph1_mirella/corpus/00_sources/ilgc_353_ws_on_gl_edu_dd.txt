- Asset-based: The program seeks to increase graduation rates.


- Deficit-based: These students have limited English proficiency.
- Asset-based: My students are bilingual and emerging English learners.

Non-inclusive and positive & affirming examples:

- Non-inclusive: Transgendered or Transsexual
- Positive & affirming: Transgender, Trans, or person of trans experience

- Non-inclusive: Homosexual
- Positive & affirming: Gay, Lesbian, Queer, etc.

- Non-inclusive: Any student who does not complete his or her application before the deadline will be charged a late fee.
- Positive & affirming: Any student who does not complete their application before the deadline will be charged a late fee.


- Non-inclusive: Gay community (when used as an umbrella term)
- Positive & affirming: LGBTQ+ community

- Non-inclusive: What are your preferred pronouns?
- Positive & affirming: What are your pronouns?


- Non-inclusive: Preferred pronouns
- Positive & affirming: Pronouns


- Non-inclusive: Preferred name
- Positive & affirming: Name


- Non-inclusive: Sexual preference
- Positive & affirming: Sexual orientation

- Positive & affirming: Chinese American (no hyphen)

- Non-inclusive: Savage
- Positive & affirming: Do not use. This word has long been used to dehumanize and harm Native Americans and Indigenous people. It should not ever be used.


- Non-inclusive: Civilized
- Positive & affirming: Do not use. This word is often used to imply other groups lack sophistication or development. It can also be used to police the response and behavior of others (i.e. "I was trying to have a civilized conversation").
