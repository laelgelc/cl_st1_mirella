How can we make a change?

When talking to or about people with SUD, make sure to use words that aren't stigmatizing. See the table below for some helpful tips to get started.

Use person-first language, which focuses on the person—not their illness. It focuses on removing words that define a person by their condition or have negative meanings. For example, "person with a substance use disorder" has a neutral tone and separates the person from his or her disorder.

Let people choose how they are described. If you're not sure what words to use, just ask! Check in with friends or loved ones about how they refer to themselves and how they would like others to refer to them.
