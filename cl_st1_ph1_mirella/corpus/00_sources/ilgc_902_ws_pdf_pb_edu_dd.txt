For example, rather than using "policeman/policewoman", use police officer, which is a gender inclusive form of this noun.

Other examples include:

Humankind or humans, rather than mankind
Artificial, rather than man-made
Spokesperson, rather than spokesman
Chairperson, rather than chairman
Server, rather than waiter/waitress
Flight Attendant, rather than stewardess
Firefighter, rather than fireman
Postal Service Worker, rather than postman

Rather than using binary-reinforcing or gendered language, like "guys", try inclusive terms like: "people", "folks", or "everyone".

Other examples include:

Caregiver/parent, rather than mom/dad
Spouse/partner, rather than husband/wife
Sibling, rather than brother/sister
Folks/everyone, rather than ladies and gentlemen
To _____, rather than "Dear Sir or Madam"
