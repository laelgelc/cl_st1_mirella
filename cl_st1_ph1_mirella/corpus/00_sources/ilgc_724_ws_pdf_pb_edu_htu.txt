Important to note, the Inclusive Style and Writing Guide provides general guidelines for language use but does not include a glossary of terms. Please refer to the Minnesota State Terms of Equity and Inclusion for information on recommended terms.

As part of the Minnesota State Brand Identity Manual, the Inclusive Writing Style Guide is intended for communications professionals and system office employees and to serve as a resource and model for our colleges and universities. Many academic disciplines have their own writing style guides and we recommend reviewing these guides through an equity lens and incorporating inclusive language principles.
