
A biased term is a word or phrase that promotes a preconceived opinion or prejudice. The following biased terms should be avoided.

Gender biased: Miss, Mrs. — Gender sensitive: Ms.

Gender biased: Men and ladies — Gender sensitive: Men and women / Women and men.

Gender biased: Man and wife — Gender sensitive: Wife and Husband / Husband and wife.

Gender biased: Girl, lasses and ladies — Gender sensitive: Woman (use "woman" if reference is to an adult).

Gender biased: The girl at the reception desk will be able to answer your questions — Gender sensitive: Our receptionist will be able to answer your questions.

Gender biased: Mr. and Mrs. John Smith — Gender sensitive: Jane and John Smith, Mr. and Mrs. Smith, Mr. and Ms. Smith.

Gender biased: Chairman — Gender sensitive: Chairperson, chair, president, presiding officer.


Gender biased: Social Good Summit attendees and their wives are invited. — Gender sensitive: Social Good Summit attendees and their partners are invited.

Gender biased: John and Mary both have full time jobs; he helps her with the housework. — Gender sensitive: John and Mary both have full time jobs; they share the housework.

Gender biased: Research scientists often neglect their wives and children. — Gender sensitive: Research scientists often neglect their families.

Gender biased: The average construction worker is experiencing impacts on his health due to hazardous working conditions by the age of 35 years. — Gender sensitive: Recent research revealed that construction workers are experiencing impacts on their health due to hazardous working conditions by the age of 35 years.

Gender biased: Each employee will do better if he has a voice in the decision. — Gender sensitive: Employees will do better if they have a voice in the decision.

Gender biased: Listen to the employee as he uses his short, simple sentences to communicate. — Gender sensitive: Listen to the employee who is using short, simple sentences to communicate.

Gender biased: Everyone must do his part. — Gender sensitive: Everyone must do their part.

Gender biased: The employer will address the situation when he is ready. — Gender sensitive: The employer will address the situation when ready.

Gender biased: The individual is strongly influenced by his family's values. — Gender sensitive: As individuals, we are strongly influenced by our families' values.

Gender biased: Each child was to write an essay on his favorite hobby. — Gender sensitive: Each child was to write an essay on his or her favorite hobby.

Gender biased: Ladies and Gentlemen. — Gender sensitive: Colleagues, delegates, or members of the association.

Gender biased: Each driver must be sure to renew his automobile license yearly. — Gender sensitive: Be sure to renew your automobile license yearly.

Gender biased: A director should require an estimate before he hires a consultant. — Gender sensitive: As a director, one should require an estimate before hiring a consultant.

Gender biased: When an editor revises a document, he usually marks his changes in red. — Gender sensitive alternatives: When revising a document, an editor usually marks changes in red. / An editor who is revising a document usually marks changes in red. / Editors usually mark their changes in red.
