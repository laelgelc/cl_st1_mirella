 Avoid                    Preferred
    -----------------------  --------------------------------------------
    disease, illness         condition, disability
    suffering from autism    (avoid; use neutral description)
    disorder                 acceptable when referring to the diagnosis of
                             autism spectrum disorder [ASD]

    Term                                    When to use
    --------------------------------------  ---------------------------------------
    "non-autistic" or "person without       if you've screened for and ruled out an
    autism"                                 ASD diagnosis
    "neurotypical"/"typically developing"   if you have thoroughly ruled out other
                                            neurodevelopmental diagnoses
    "general population"                    to refer to community participants who
                                            may or may not have neurodevelopmental
                                            diagnoses
    "comparison group"                      with a description of relevant
                                            characteristics


    Avoid              Preferred
    -----------------  ----------------------------------------
    high functioning   autism without intellectual disability
    low functioning    autism with intellectual disability



    Avoid         Preferred
    ------------  ----------------------------------------------------
    at risk       increased/elevated likelihood, correlated with a
                  diagnosis of autism
    risk factor   associated factor, correlate, predictor


    Avoid              Preferred
    -----------------  --------------------
    nonverbal          non-speaking
    minimally verbal   minimally-speaking


    Avoid              Preferred
    -----------------  ----------------------------------------------------
    autism symptoms    autistic traits, autism characteristics, features of
    autism severity    autism



    Avoid       Preferred
    ----------  -------------------------------------
    comorbid    co-existing, co-occurring, secondary
