People, Y'all, You Folks
Everyone Gender inclusive way to address a group. 

Person who is temporarily able-bodied
Description/Explanation: To describe people who do not currently have a physical disability

Cisgender
Description/Explanation: A person whose gender identity aligns with the sex they were assigned at birth

Trans, transgender
Description/Explanation: A person whose gender identity is different than the one they were assigned at birth

Genderqueer
Description/Explanation: A term under the trans umbrella which refers to people who identify outside of the false male-female binary. Genderqueer people may experience erasure if they are perceived as cisgender. Genderqueer folks who are perceived as genderqueer are often subjected to gender policing.

Non-binary
Description/Explanation: A person whose gender identity does not align exclusively with a false male/female gender binary

Gender Non-Conforming
Description/Explanation: Refers to people who do not conform to society's expectation for their gender roles or gender expression. Some prefer 'gender variant'

Gender-fluid
Description/Explanation: A person who does not identify with a single and fixed gender

Bisexual
Description/Explanation: A person who is sexually or emotionally attracted to more than one sex or gender

Queer
Description/Explanation: An umbrella term used to describe the diverse ways in which people reject the binary categories of gender and/or sexual orientation. Also an academic term to reject the colonization into any classification mechanisms.


Intersex
Description/Explanation: A person born with sex characteristics such as genitalia or chromosomes that do not fit the biological criteria for a male or female binary


Pansexual
Description/Explanation: A person who can be attracted to a variety of people regardless of their biological sex or their gender identity


Asexual (sometimes shortened to Ace)
Description/Explanation: A person who does not experience sexual attraction to other people


Aromantic (sometimes shortened to Aro)
Description/Explanation: A person who does not experience romantic attraction to other people

Economically marginalized, person of low socioeconomic status (SES)
Term to describe people who are pushed to the margins of the economy

Partner, spouse
Gender inclusive way of referring to a romantic partner
