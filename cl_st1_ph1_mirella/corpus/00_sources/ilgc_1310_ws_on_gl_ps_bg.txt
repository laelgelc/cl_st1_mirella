INTRODUCTION

Inclusive language from clinicians and staff helps to make patients feel
empowered in the health care setting. It works to improve feelings of isolation
and discrimination based on their social identities including, but not limited
to, their race or ethnicity, nationality, sexual orientation, gender, age,
ability, or socioeconomic status.

Effective communication has been linked to improving patient engagement,
adherence to treatment, satisfaction, quality of life, and reduced psychological
symptoms (e.g. depression and anxiety).
