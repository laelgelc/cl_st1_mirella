Gender identity and gender expression are two different things. Gender expression is how somebody outwardly presents themselves to the world. Their gender identity may or may not match this. Sharing your own pronouns helps to create a safe space for people to be themselves. In an online meeting, some people share their pronouns after their name.

Language such as 'brothers and sisters', while intended to be inclusive and friendly, doesn't take into account our non-binary friends. You might consider using 'siblings', 'friends' or 'children of God' instead.

Transgender, or trans for short, means somebody is now living with a gender identity and/or expression that they were not born with, and may have changed the pronouns they wish to use. This should be respected.

The word 'queer' and its meaning has evolved over the years. It is commonly known, even in the recent past, as an offensive term for homosexual. In recent years it has been claimed by some LGBT+ people to express their sexuality positively.

Antisemitism is any belief, policy or action that discriminates against or incites hatred towards Jewish people, by either race or religion, or caricatures Jewish people and culture. This can include denying the right of Israel to exist, or judging it by standards not applied to other nations. 

Islamophobia refers to any belief, policy or action that discriminates against or incites hatred towards Muslims or which caricatures Muslim people and culture.
