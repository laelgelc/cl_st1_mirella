Inclusive Language At Work

                                   Don't Say                   Try These Instead

Talking about Staffing             We need more manpower       Let's add team members
                                                               OR
                                                               We need additional support

Gender-Neutral Terms in Writing    He/She will attend.         They will attend
                                                               OR
                                                               The employee will attend

Assumptions about Personal Life    Bring your wife/husband     Bring your partner
                                                               or
                                                               Feel free to bring a guest

Addressing Groups                  Hey guys                    Hi all
                                                               or
                                                               Hello team

For example, using "low-income" rather than "poor", or "undocumented immigrant" rather than "illegal immigrant".
