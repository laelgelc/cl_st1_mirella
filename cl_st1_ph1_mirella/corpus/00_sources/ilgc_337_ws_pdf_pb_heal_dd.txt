QUICK REFERENCE GUIDE

AGE

Item 1:
- Instead of: Under 1, 1-9, 10-19, 20-34, 35-54, 55-65
- Use: Under 16, 16-19, 20-24, 25-29, 30-34, 35-39, 40-44, 45-49, 50-54, 55-59, 60-64, 65-69, 70-74, 75+

Item 2:
- Instead of: The elderly / Aged / Old people / Senior citizen / Pensioner
- Use: Older person / Older people / Older citizens / Older adult

Item 3:
- Instead of: [XX] many years' experience / Mature / Young
- Use: Words relating to the desired attributes of an applicant: proven experience / adaptable / enthusiastic person / reliable / good communication skills etc.

Item 4:
- Instead of: How would you feel about managing older/younger people?
- Use: What skills do you have to enable you to effectively manage a team?


DISABILITY

Item 1:
- Instead of: The disabled / The blind / Wheelchair-bound / An asthmatic
- Use: Disabled people / People with visual impairments, people who are partially sighted / Wheelchair user / An asthmatic person

Item 2:
- Instead of: Spastic / Mongol / Mentally handicapped
- Use: Person with cerebral palsy / Person with Down's syndrome / Person with learning difficulties

Item 3:
- Instead of: Special arrangements have been made to accommodate deaf people / Please let the organisers know if you cannot use stairs
- Use: Please let us know if you require any assistance to enable you to attend/participate. If you would like to discuss your access requirements further, please contact... / The facilities are equipped with loop hearing systems

Item 4:
- Instead of: Disabled parking space / Disabled toilets / Disabled lifts etc
- Use: Accessible parking spaces / Accessible toilets / Accessible lifts etc

Item 5:
- Instead of: Posing questions to others, eg would they like some coffee? Does he take sugar?
- Use: Speak to the individual themselves, eg would you like some coffee?

Item 6:
- Instead of: Speaking slowly and assuming low intelligence
- Use: Speak in your normal manner and tone

Item 7:
- Instead of: How would you feel about managing older/younger people?
- Use: What skills do you have to enable you to effectively manage a team?


GENDER

Item 1:
- Instead of: Dear Mr [NAME] / Dear Sir
- Use: Dear Ms/Mr [NAME] if you know the family name / Dear Sir/Madam if you don't know the family name

Item 2:
- Instead of: Dear Ms/Mr [NAME]
- Use: Dear [FIRST NAME] [FAMILY NAME], eg Dear Chris Jones

Item 3:
- Instead of: Prof / Dr / Mr / Mrs / Miss / Ms / Mx etc (in a perceived hierarchy)
- Use: Dr / Miss / Mr / Mrs / Ms / Mx / Prof etc (in alphabetical order)

Item 4:
- Instead of: The lecturer will display his timetable on his office door / Each student is responsible for material on loan to him
- Use: Lecturers will display their timetables on their office doors / Students are responsible for material they borrow

Item 5:
- Instead of: Anyone who wants his work evaluated
- Use: Anyone who wants their work evaluated

Item 6:
- Instead of: Man or mankind / Man-hours / Manpower
- Use: Humanity, humans, human beings, people, or society / Work hours or staff time / Staff, workforce, personnel, workers

Item 7:
- Instead of: Girls / Ladies / Dear / Son / Love
- Use: The person's name, their professional title or 'men' and 'women'

Item 8:
- Instead of: A female doctor / A male nurse
- Use: A doctor / A nurse

Item 9:
- Instead of: Miss/Mrs
- Use: Ms - This term is intended to parallel Mr as it does not identify marital status

Item 10:
- Instead of: Chairman / Policeman / Cleaning ladies
- Use: Chair, chairperson / Police officer / Cleaners


GENDER REASSIGNMENT

Item 1:
- Instead of: A transsexual
- Use: Transsexual/transgender person or trans person

Item 2:
- Instead of: She or her (for a trans-male, ie female to male) / He or his (for a trans-female, ie male to female)
- Use: He or his (for a trans-male, ie female to male) / She or her (for a trans-female, ie male to female)

Item 3:
- Instead of: Biologically male/female / Genetically male/female / Born a man/woman
- Use: Assigned male/female at birth / Designated male/female at birth

Item 4:
- Instead of: Sex change/sex change operation / Pre-operative / Post-operative
- Use: Transition. Note: referring to a sex-change operation, or using terms such as pre-operative or post-operative inaccurately suggests that one must have surgery in order to transition.


MARRIAGE AND CIVIL PARTNERSHIP

Item 1:
- Instead of: Asking someone's marital status or specifying marital status options: single / married / divorced / widow/widower
- Use: What is your relationship status? or What is your marital/civil partnership status? Relationship status options: single / married/civil partner / divorced/dissolved civil partnership / widow/widower/surviving civil partner


PREGNANCY AND MATERNITY

Item 1:
- Instead of: Expectant mothers
- Use: Pregnant people


RACE (INCLUDING ETHNIC OR NATIONAL ORIGINS, COLOUR AND NATIONALITY)

Item 1:
- Instead of: The Asians etc...
- Use: Asian students etc...

Item 2:
- Instead of: A Chinese professor
- Use: A professor

Item 3:
- Instead of: Coloured people / Black/Asian when referring to all minority ethnic groups
- Use: The term preferred by the individual, eg Asian, Afro-Caribbean, Black etc


RELIGION OR BELIEF

Item 1:
- Instead of: Christian name / Surname / Last name
- Use: First name, given name, forename or personal / Family name (preferred term)


SEXUAL ORIENTATION

Item 1:
- Instead of: The blanket term homosexual
- Use: Gay, lesbian, bisexual, gay man, gay woman. Use all these terms to describe these groups/individuals. LGB (lesbian, gay and bisexual) is a common acronym used for the collective term.

Item 2:
- Instead of: Lesbians, gays or bisexuals
- Use: People who are lesbian, gay or bisexual

Item 3:
- Instead of: Husband, wife and spouse / Girlfriend or boyfriend / Family planning clinic
- Use: Partner or accompanying person (so as to not discriminate between married, unmarried or same sex partners) / Partner (or term preferred by the individual) / Sexual health clinic or sexual health and wellbeing clinic
