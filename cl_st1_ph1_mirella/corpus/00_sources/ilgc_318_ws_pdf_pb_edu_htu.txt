These guiding principles aim to connect you to the work while staying grounded in a solutions-based approach. Examples are given, but our goal is not to create a comprehensive list of what not to say; instead, we invite you to take this opportunity to reflect on what you want to contribute to. As language continues to evolve, the point is not to get it right all of the time, but to be intentional and indicate to those around us that we are sensitive to, and respect different identities.
