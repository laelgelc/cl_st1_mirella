Note: 
As culture changes, language changes as well. Therefore, this guide will be reviewed periodically to ensure the appropriate and correct terms are included and changed as needed. Please note, some of the preferential terms used in this guide are based on the preferences of specific communities and individuals and are subject to change.

Language is powerful and can be used as a tool of empowerment or discrimination. Being mindful and considerate when choosing the words you use is a great first step to using inclusive language. Think before you speak and ask when you are unsure!
