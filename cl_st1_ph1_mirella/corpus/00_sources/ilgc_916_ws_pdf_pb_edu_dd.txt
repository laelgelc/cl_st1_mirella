If you see the terms "sexual preference" or "lifestyle choice," replace them with "sexual orientation" or "gender identity." 

'Suffers from': try 'lives with' or 'has' (i.e. 'Jean has cystic fibrosis').

'Disabled' toilets or facilities: 'accessible' is the best term ('ambulant' is sometimes used, but can be problematic as it literally means 'able to walk around').

'Wheelchair-bound': wheelchairs liberate and enable mobility; they do not confine. 'Wheelchair user' is more appropriate when access needs to be highlighted.
