Introduction

Our goal at OMNI Institute (OMNI) is to intentionally center equity in every stage of our projects. Centering equity requires a commitment to understanding the complex nature and nuances that result from existing in a society where not just one kind of community lives, operates, and functions. A significant part of this comes from language and how we utilize language. In this guide, we provide best and emerging practices for inclusive language. We use inclusive language as a best practice for the following reasons:

Reach: To reach as many participants as possible. Inclusive language better ensures we get complete data from all community members, as individuals from non-dominant identities are more likely to participate in our research and evaluation projects when they see themselves reflected.

Data Quality: To improve data clarity. We have found that by using inclusive language, our data quality improves, whereas removing these options or using other wording can increase confusion or reduce the reliability of our findings.

Respect: To ensure that all participants feel seen, heard, and valued. Ultimately, we use inclusive language as we live in diverse communities where individuals have complex and intersectional identities and experiences. We want to better ensure that we treat all our participants with dignity and empathy.

Language is ever changing, so this guide serves as a living document that will shift and adapt alongside cultural changes and new inclusive language practices.

Considerations When Thinking of Language

An article titled Why Inclusive Language Matters, published by the American Psychological Association (APA), opens with a quote  which states, "Language is power, in ways more literal than most people think." It's important to recognize that language not only impacts the way we communicate with each other, but it also impacts the manner in which we are able to reach each other. In OMNI's case, our usage of language greatly impacts how we convey the story that our clients are trusting us to tell.

About OMNI Institute

OMNI Institute is a nonprofit social science consultancy that provides integrated research, evaluation, and capacity-building services to foster understanding, guide collaboration, and inform action to accelerate positive social change. We work in partnership with our clients – leaders and change-makers in governments, nonprofits, foundations, and social enterprises – to accelerate change and improve lives. Our primary areas of expertise are Behavioral Health, Community Health, Children & Families, Economic Security, and Justice, and we operate by four core values: Inquiry to uncover the best possible solutions to challenges, Agility in our approach, Accountability for our work, and a true Connection to our clients and the communities they serve.
