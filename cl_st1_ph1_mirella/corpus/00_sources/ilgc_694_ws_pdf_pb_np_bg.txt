INTRODUCTION


The words we use to talk about lesbian, gay, bisexual and transgender (LGBTQ) people and issues can have a powerful impact on our conversations, particularly when it comes to talking with those who are not as familiar with LGBTQ lives. The right words can help open people's hearts and minds, while other words can create distance or confusion.

Designed for new allies who often face a confusing array of terminology and descriptions, this short guide offers an overview of essential vocabulary to use and avoid. For messages and approaches that can be used to talk about various issues, see MAP's online collection of messaging guides and resources at www.lgbtmap.org/messaging-guides.
