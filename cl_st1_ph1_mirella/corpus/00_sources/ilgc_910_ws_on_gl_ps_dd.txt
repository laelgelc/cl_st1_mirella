Inclusive Language List for Different Demographics

Instead of using: Guys (for a mixed group) | Try writing: Everyone, folks
Instead of using: Girls (for adults), females | Try writing: Women
Instead of using: Ladies and gentlemen, men and women | Try writing: Everyone, friends, colleagues
Instead of using: She/he | Try writing: They (unless pronoun known)
Instead of using: Minorities | Try writing: People of color, marginalized, or underrepresented groups
Instead of using: Elderly people | Try writing: Older adults, adults
Instead of using: Chairman (and other gender-biased titles) | Try writing: Chairperson
Instead of using: Powwow | Try writing: Meeting, get together
Instead of using: Normal (when referring to sexual identity) | Try writing: Cisgender
Instead of using: Transgender (as a noun) | Try writing: Transgender person
Instead of using: Straight | Try writing: Non-LGBTQ
Instead of using: Homosexual | Try writing: Lesbian or gay person
Instead of using: Lifestyle, preference (not referring to hobbies and interests) | Try writing: Orientation, identity

Inclusive Language List for Health and Ability

Instead of using: Disabled/handicapped person | Try writing: Person with a disability
Instead of using: Wheelchair-bound | Try writing: Person who uses a wheelchair
Instead of using: Suffering from | Try writing: Living with
Instead of using: Addict | Try writing: Someone struggling with addiction
Instead of using: Crazy | Try writing: Person with a mental illness
Instead of using: Crazy (not referring to mental illness) | Try writing: Unbelievable, over the top
Instead of using: Lame | Try writing: Person with injury or disability
Instead of using: Lame (not referring to an injury) | Try writing: Uninspired, boring

Instead of using: Wife, husband, boyfriend, girlfriend | Try writing: Partner
Instead of using: Mothering, fathering | Try writing: Parenting
Instead of using: Parent | Try writing: Caregiver
Instead of using: Christmas party | Try writing: Holiday party
Instead of using: Tribe (for social group) | Try writing: Friendship circle
Instead of using: Normal (not referring to gender identity) | Try writing: Typical
