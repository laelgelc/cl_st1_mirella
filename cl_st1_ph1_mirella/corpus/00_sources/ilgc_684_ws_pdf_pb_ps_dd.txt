COMMON EXCLUSIONARY LANGUAGE & INCLUSIVE ALTERNATIVES

There are no definitive rules or static guidelines around inclusive language. Specific recommendations are fluid — subject to evolve along with our understanding of language and how it impacts different audiences. With that said, these are some common examples of non-inclusive language, and corresponding situational alternatives you might consider.

Instead of this | Consider

Salesman, Chairman, Mankind | Salesperson, Chair, Humankind

Males, Females | Men, Women, People

Both genders | All genders

You guys | Everyone, Folks, Y'all

Blacks, Mexicans, Asians | Person who is Black/Mexican/Asian

Blacklist, Whitelist | Deny list, Allow list

Grandfathering, Grandfather clause | Legacy

Differently abled, Disabled, Handicapped | Person with a disability

Crazy, Insane, Nuts | Wild, Unbelievable, Strange

Turn a blind eye, Tone-deaf | Ignore, Insensitive

Addict, Alcoholic | Person with an addiction/alcoholism

The elderly | Older adults
