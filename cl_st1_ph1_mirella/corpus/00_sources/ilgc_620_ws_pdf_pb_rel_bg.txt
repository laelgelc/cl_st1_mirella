INCLUSIVE LANGUAGE POLICY

The IHM Congregation is committed to fostering right relationship with God, among ourselves, with the Church and the whole Earth community in all we do in word and action. Therefore, in all IHM common prayer, including Eucharistic liturgies, the utmost care and attention will be given to the use of inclusive language patterns. These patterns will be gender-inclusive when using language for the human person, both gender inclusive and expansive when speaking of God, and attentive to the reality of our evolving cosmic consciousness. (Adopted April 7, 2016 IHM Coordinating Council)
