At Charity Bank, we respect individual preferences and use the terminology chosen by the individuals themselves.

At Charity Bank we strive to achieve equality through promoting equity.

At Charity Bank we view 'Diversity' and 'Representation' as separate concepts and use 'Diversity' as a preferred term.

At Charity Bank we recognise that communities are prevented from accessing the services and resources they need due to systemic bias and disadvantage.

At Charity Bank, we engage with people of all faiths and none. It is important to be aware of the different ways that people might identify their religion or belief.
