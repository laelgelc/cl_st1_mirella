For instance, 'Rebecca is a Kaurna woman' instead of 'Rebecca is an Aboriginal woman'. Indeed, this reference should only be made when relevant to the context and after given consent by the individual(s).

Referring to Older People
What not to say: Old people, the elderly
What to say: Older people, retired people, retirees, older Australians (or other nationality), people aged between X and Y.

Referring to Younger People
What not to say: Young, juniors, youths
What to say: Younger people, youth, younger Australians (or other nationality), people aged between X and Y.

Additionally, avoid using job titles that end in '-man' or '-woman' (for example, instead of saying policeman – use 'police officer' instead).

Preferred Language: People with diverse genders, sexes and sexualities; LGBTQIA+
Language to Avoid: The gay community
