Looking Ahead

This guide is not exhaustive, and PCMCH and BORN recognize that language is continually evolving. While this version does not cover socioeconomic status, religion and spirituality, body image and size diversity, neurodiversity, age and aging, and other important areas, we acknowledge their significance and plan to consider them in future updates. The inclusive language framework and guiding principles outlined in this guide provide a valuable foundation for choosing appropriate terminology in these contexts. As we move forward, we encourage ongoing dialogue and feedback to enhance our understanding and application of inclusive language within perinatal and child healthcare. Together, we can foster environments that respect and honor the diversity of all individuals, ultimately enhancing communication and care in our communities.
