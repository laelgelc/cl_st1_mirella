For example, use low socioeconomic status rather than lower class. 

Use people who are systemically excluded or institutionally oppressed, rather than minority.

- Instead of saying pregnant women, say pregnant people.
- Instead of women's health rights, say reproductive rights.
- Instead of feminine hygiene products, say menstrual products or period products.
- Instead of breast feeding, say chest feeding.

Instead of, A resident should take out his or her recycling, say, Residents should take out their recycling.

- Instead of saying sexual preference, say sexual orientation.
- Instead of gay lifestyle, say gay lives.
- Instead of homosexual, say gay man, lesbian, gay person.

Avoid the dehumanizing collective noun the homeless, instead, use people-first language: people experiencing homelessness, people experiencing houselessness, people without housing, or people without homes.

Use the phrase died by suicide. Avoid the phrase committed suicide, which evokes associations with committing a crime or a sin. 

Don't call groups of people guys. Good alternatives for addressing groups include: colleagues, friends, folks, everyone.
