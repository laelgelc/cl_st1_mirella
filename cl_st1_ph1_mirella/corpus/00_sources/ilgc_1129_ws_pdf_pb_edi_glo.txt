Terms to use with care

"minority" vs. "person of color"
"Person of color" refers to all racial and ethnic groups that are not white/European. This is the preferred term when using "minority" would be a demographic inaccuracy, like in an environment where POC outnumber whites.

"Latina/o" vs. "Hispanic"
"Latina/o" refers to people in the United States with Latin American ancestry, while "Hispanic" refers to those from Spanish-speaking countries. Latino is an ethnic group, not a race category.
Often, police circulate "Hispanic" as a description. If used in that context, make sure to attribute the "Hispanic" description to police. Better yet, write a physical description with as many other details as possible.

"Latinx"
The term, pronounced "La-teen-ex," is an alternative to Latino or Latina. It refers to people who don't identify as female or male.

"Nazi" or "nazi"
By definition, a Nazi is a member of the National Socialist German Workers Party. When used outside of this context, it minimizes the suffering and death of millions at the hands of Nazis during WWII.
