Transgender: An adjective to describe people whose gender does not match the one usually associated with the sex they were assigned at birth. 

Native American: A member of any of the first groups of people living in North America. (Other terms: American Indian, First Nation or Indigenous person.)

Latino, Latina, Latinx: Often a preferred noun or adjective for a person from, or whose ancestors were from, a Spanish-speaking land or culture or from Latin America. "Latina" is the feminine form, and "Latino" the masculine form. Some prefer the gender-neutral "Latinx," which should be accompanied by a short explanation: Gomez prefers the gender-neutral term Latinx.

Hispanic: A person from, or whose ancestors were from, a Spanish-speaking land or culture. Latino, Latina or Latinx are sometimes preferred. Follow the person's preference. Use a more specific identification when possible, such as Brazilian, Puerto Rican or Mexican American.

Spanish: Refers to someone who is from Spain.
