
- Here are some examples of how to make more inclusive use of language:

    Word or Phrase                        Alternative
    ------------------------------------  ------------------------------------
    Each associate should read his copy   Associates should read their copies
    of document review parameters         of the document review parameters
    carefully.                            carefully.

    Whoever bills the most hours this     Whoever bills the most hours this
    year will have his or her billable    year will get a billable rate
    rate increased.                       increase.

    Please feel free to invite your       Please feel free to invite a
    husband or boyfriend to the firm's    spouse(s) or partner(s) to the firm's
    holiday party.                        holiday party.

    Women are encouraged to apply.        All genders are encouraged to apply.

    Dear Ms. Smith:                       Dear Taylor Smith:

    John Doe v. Company                   Person A v. Company
    Jane Doe v. Company
