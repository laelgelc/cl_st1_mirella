GSMA — INCLUSIVE LANGUAGE GUIDANCE
February 2025

The GSMA aims to maintain and promote a work environment that is representative of the rich diversity of our employees. We achieve this through celebrating diversity globally, helping everyone understand what it means to be inclusive and ensuring that equity is at the heart of everything we do.

The GSMA aims to create the conditions whereby people are treated fairly on the basis of their merits and potential; regardless of their race, nationality, gender, gender identity, age, marital status, sexual orientation, disability, socioeconomic background, religious or political belief, caste or tribe, or military status.

INTRODUCTION

What is inclusive language?

Inclusive language is a form of communication that avoids using words, expressions or assumptions that would stereotype, demean or exclude people. For example, gendered language is commonly understood as language that has a bias towards a particular sex or social gender. In English, this would include using gender-specific terms referring to professions or people, such as businessman or fireman.

Please note the terms used in this guide reflect the diverse identities and experiences of British and American society, there might be alternative preferred terms in other regions which we look forward to capturing.

Language is powerful and can have varying effects, the issue is that language changes, it evolves, and with the best intentions, you can't get it right all the time. Changing personal habits needs both patience and empathy. This guide provides information to help all GSMA employees use inclusive language with confidence.

Why use inclusive language?

For individuals within marginalised groups, inclusive language indicates that this is a safe space where authenticity is welcomed. It signifies that inclusivity is a priority where every voice is significant and diverse opinions are valued.

For example, the use of pronouns is not just about clarifying which pronouns we use; it demonstrates our support as allies to the LGBTQIA+ community. The adoption of such language signals safe and inclusive environment.
