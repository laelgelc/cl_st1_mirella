Below are a few examples.

Exclusive → Inclusive
Manpower (implies only men contribute) → Workforce or human resources
Confined to a wheelchair (focuses on the limitation) → Uses a wheelchair (focuses on the tool)
Homeless (dehumanizing) → Experiencing homelessness
Orientals (outdated term for people from Asia) → People of Asian descent
Chairman (implies only men can lead) → Chair or chairperson

For example, instead of "sir" or "ma'am," use
"friend" or simply address the person by name. Use terms like "partner" instead of
"boyfriend/girlfriend," "child" instead of "son/daughter."

e.g., using a wheelchair vs. wheelchair-bound.
