General Principles for Avoiding Bias

1. Describe people at the appropriate level of specificity. Focus on relevant characteristics.

2. Be sensitive to labels. Call people what they call themselves. Avoid derogatory language.

An Inclusive Mindset

Don’t make assumptions. Everyone has a unique perspective.

Impact matters more than intention. Listen to others.

Don’t expect perfection. Do your best to be inclusive.

Topic: Age
Guideline: Show aging is a normal part of the human experience, separate from disease and disorder.

Topic: Disability
Guideline: Use person-first or identity-first language according to the preferences of the person/group. Avoid euphemistic and fatalistic language.

Topic: Gender
Guideline: Use gender-inclusive language and self-identified pronouns. Avoid assuming a gender binary.

Topic: Race and ethnicity
Guideline: Ask people to self-identify if possible because the concept of race is not universal. Capitalize “Black” and “White.”

Topic: Sexual orientation
Guideline: Use the umbrella terms “sexual orientation and gender diversity” and “sexual and gender minorities.”

Topic: Socioeconomic status
Guideline: Emphasize what people have, not what they lack.
