Inclusive Language Guide

ABOUT THIS RESOURCE


Please note that the explanations in this document are distinct and separate from how these words are defined and interpreted under law.

These guidelines aim to raise awareness, guide learning, and support the use of culturally sensitive terms and phrases that center the voices and perspectives of those who are often marginalized or stereotyped. They also explain the origins for problematic terms and phrases and offer suitable alternatives or more contemporary replacements. This document will be flexible and iterative in nature, continuing to evolve as new terminology emerges or current language
becomes obsolete.
