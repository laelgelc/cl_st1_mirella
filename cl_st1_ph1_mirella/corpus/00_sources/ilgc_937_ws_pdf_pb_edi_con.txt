Conclusion

School nurses serve as influential role models within their school communities, setting a significant example for others to follow. When providing care to school children and collaborating with their caregivers, it is critical to foster inclusivity and belonging. One method of being inclusive involves eliminating the use of biased language by acknowledging implicit biases, using person-first language per individual student preferences, practicing cultural humility, calling in, and using correct pronouns. Inclusive language is a powerful tool for school nurses when cultivating a climate of belonging and acceptance. Using inclusive language decreases implicit bias, prejudice, and stigma, all of which contribute to health care inequity and injustice. School nurses may make mistakes in their practice; however, a commitment to continuous learning demonstrates that fostering inclusivity is an ongoing journey throughout one's life. When school nurses make a commitment to foster the thoughtful recognition of inclusive language, they initiate discussions that promote awareness and advocacy and support belonging.
