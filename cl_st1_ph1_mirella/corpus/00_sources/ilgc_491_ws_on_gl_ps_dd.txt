For example, "person with disability" is preferred over "disabled person" or "handicapped person." "person with HIV" is preferred over "AIDS victim."

Gender
* Gendered Language: Chairman/chairwoman -> Gender Neutral Language: Chairperson, chair
* Gendered Language: Manpower -> Gender Neutral Language: Workforce, workers
* Gendered Language: Manned -> Gender Neutral Language: Crewed

Disability or Neurodiversity
* Abelist Language: Wheelchair-bound -> Inclusive Alternatives: Person who uses a wheelchair
* Abelist Language: Disabled or handicapped -> Inclusive Alternatives: Person with disability, person with a physical disability
* Abelist Language: Special needs -> Inclusive Alternatives: Person with disability, differently abled

Socio-economic Status
* Classist Language: The poor / Poor people -> Socioeconomically-Sensitive Terms: People with incomes below the federal poverty threshold/low-income people
* Classist Language: Blue-collar worker -> Socioeconomically-Sensitive Terms: Skilled trades worker / manual laborer
* Classist Language: Ghetto / the ghetto -> Socioeconomically-Sensitive Terms: Under-resourced area / low socio-economic area
