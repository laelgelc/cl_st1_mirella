
Here are some examples demonstrating how to change your language to be as inclusive as possible:

Instead of saying: The blind leading the blind
Try saying this: They don't seem to know what they are doing

Instead of saying: Confined to a wheelchair
Try saying this: She uses a wheelchair for mobility

Instead of saying: They are deaf to reason
Try saying this: They are unresponsive to reason

As a general rule of thumb, we should avoid using masculine or feminine language. For example, using "they" as a singular pronoun rather than 'he' or 'she'.

For example:

- 'A person from the Irish Traveller community' rather than 'an Irish Traveller'
- 'A person from the South Asian community' rather than 'a South Asian person'
- 'A person from the Caribbean community' rather than 'a Caribbean person'
