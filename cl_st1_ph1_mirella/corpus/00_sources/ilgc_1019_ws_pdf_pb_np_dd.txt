1: Avoid calling it 'Autism Spectrum Disorder', ASD or ASC
Call it 'autism', or just talk about 'being autistic'

2: Say 'autistic person' instead of 'person with autism'
(…unless the person you are talking to has told you otherwise).

Replacing pathologizing language

If the autistic person you are talking to expresses specific preferences, then please use those. In all other cases, choose the following.
