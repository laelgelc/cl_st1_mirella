Some people with a disability reject the term ‘able-bodied’ because it implies people with a disability lack able bodies. People with a disability who use identity-first language, however, tend to use ‘abled’ to describe people without disability, and ‘able-bodied’ to describe people without physical or mobility-related disability for the purposes of consistency.
