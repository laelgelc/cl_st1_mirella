Here are more ways you can adjust your gendered language to be more inclusive:

Gendered Language | Gender-Neutral Language

Fireman, Policeman | Firefighter, Police officer
Chairman, chairwoman | Chair
Actor/actress | Actor
Mankind | Humanity, people, human beings
Male nurse, female doctor | Nurse, doctor
'The best man for the job'. | 'The best person for the job'.
'Guys' or other gendered collective phrases to refer to a group of people | Everyone, folks, people, you all, etc.

e.g. 'firefighters' instead of 'firemen'

Using 'systemically marginalised people' instead of 'minority' acknowledges the reality of how certain groups were oppressed and treated by dominant groups.
