How this guide works
This guide seeks to empower individuals and organisations to communicate in a way that is respectful, inclusive, and reflective of the diverse identities that exist in society. It offers practical tools to ensure that everyone — regardless of their background or circumstances — feels seen, heard and valued.
This guide provides a non-exhaustive list of inclusive communication recommendations. It also features a dedicated section on common words and phrases to avoid and guidance on LGBTIQA+ flags, symbols and visual representation.
This guide builds on knowledge of key terms related to LGBTIQA+ communities. Definitions of key terms can also be found in a range of other documents, such as the ACT Government's Common Dataset: Sex, Gender, Variations of Sex Characteristics and Sexual Orientation Variables Data.
This guide is not prescriptive but rather provides a range of suggestions to facilitate more inclusive speaking and writing. When thinking about how to refer to someone, adopt a person-centred approach. This means that, ultimately, it is up to the individual to decide how they want to be referred to.
