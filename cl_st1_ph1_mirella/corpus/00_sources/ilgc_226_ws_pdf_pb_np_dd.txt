Gender neutral language
A lot of inclusive language means removing traditionally gendered roles and using more
neutral language. Here are some ways you can be more inclusive when referring to
specific groups:

Try/ Instead of
People, everyone Ladies and gentlemen, guys, other
gendered groupings
Server Waiter/waitress
Flight attendant Steward/stewardess
Humankind Mankind
Partner, spouse Husband or wife
Spokesperson, representative Spokesman
Workers Workmen
They He or she
Parents, guardians Mothers, fathers
