TIPS AND EXAMPLES


TIP 1 - Use general terms for people


TIP 2 - Using 'they' for a generic person


TIP 3 - Rewrite sentences to avoid the need for any pronouns


TIP 4 - Avoid gendered terminology


TIP 5 - Avoid gendered addresses


TIP 6 - Avoid gendered adjectives


TIP 7 - Use, and ask for pronouns, or self-identified pronouns


TIP 8 - Differentiate between gender, sex, pronouns

SENTENCE EXAMPLES


Form: They
   Example: Cleo is French. They study politics.

Form: Them
   Example: Every client got a care package delivered to them.
            Yesterday they got a care package delivered to them.

Form: Their
   Example: Each child played with their parent.
            As a child, Cleo played with their parent.

Form: Theirs
   Example: The cup of coffee is theirs.

Form: Themselves (or themself)
   Example: A private person usually keeps to themselves [or themself].


Form: He
   Example: Mark is British. He studies economics.

Form: Him
   Example: Yesterday, Mark got a care package delivered to him.

Form: His
   Example: When he was younger, Mark played with his parent.

Form: His
   Example: The cup of coffee is his.

Form: Himself
   Example: Mark is a private person, he usually keeps to himself.


Form: She
   Example: Tay is from Kenya. She is a teacher.

Form: Her
   Example: Yesterday, Tay got a care package delivered to her.

Form: Her
   Example: When she was younger, Tay played with her parent.

Form: Hers
   Example: The cup of coffee is hers.

Form: Herself
   Example: Tay is a private person, she usually keeps to herself.
