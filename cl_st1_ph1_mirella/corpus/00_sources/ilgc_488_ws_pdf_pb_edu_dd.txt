1. Recast the sentence and make the subject and object plural.
* Example: Each student must hand in his paper by 2:00 pm on Friday.
* Instead, say: Students must hand in their papers by 2:00 pm on Friday.

2. Omit the pronoun or use an article (e.g., the, a) instead of a pronoun.
* Example: The cashier should call her manager when a customer asks to use an expired coupon.
* Instead, say: The cashier should call the manager when a customer asks to use an expired coupon.

3. Use the neutral pronoun one.
* Example: A writer in Boston is likely to earn more than she will in Syracuse.
* Instead, say: A writer in Boston is likely to earn more than one in Syracuse.

Gender-Inclusive Titles or Terms
* Anchor instead of anchorman
* Artificial instead of manmade
* Business executive instead of businessman
* Camera operator instead of cameraman
* Chair instead of chairman
* Guard or staff instead of man (v.)
* Humanity instead of mankind
* Layperson instead of layman
* Police officer instead of policeman
* Spokesperson instead of spokesman
* Workforce instead of manpower

Use relationship violence, domestic violence, dating violence, intimate partner violence, or abusive relationship rather than dispute, quarrel, and love triangle, which minimize the violence.

Use the term reported instead of accused, claimed, and alleged, which convey bias and disbelief.

Use the active voice (e.g., he raped him, he abused her, they reported that he assaulted them) rather than the passive voice (e.g., he was raped, she was abused, they were assaulted), which removes the accountability of the person who committed the behavior and blames the person harmed.
