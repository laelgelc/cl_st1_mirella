
This document provides definitions for commonly used words in health equity discourse, identifies harmful words and offers equity-centered alternatives, clarifies subtle differences between seemingly synonymous terms, and explains how certain words perpetuate racist narratives while others promote racial justice. It aims to raise awareness and encourage users to think critically about the words they use, the meaning conveyed, and the potential impact.

The MMA encourages all members, staff, and elected leaders to apply these guidelines across their internal and external communications, including when creating information or resources, giving presentations, developing promotional materials, and drafting and reviewing policies and procedures.
